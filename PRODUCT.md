# Fasthaus — Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Primary: home shoppers discovering, evaluating and buying objects for their everyday spaces.
- Secondary: customers with custom projects and trade enquiries, including design professionals and residential or commercial projects.

## Product Purpose

Help home shoppers discover and purchase Fasthaus objects. The website's primary outcome is to generate purchases; custom and trade enquiries are secondary.

## Positioning

Sculptural & functional spatial objects designed in-house and made to order in the UAE, with warm light for everyday spaces.

Lighting is the current storefront focus. Preserve the broader spatial-objects positioning rather than defining Fasthaus solely as a lamp retailer.

## Operating Context

Shoppers browse the collection, inspect product imagery, variants and specifications, add items to their cart and complete checkout. Customers can use the contact page for product questions, order support and custom-project enquiries.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Checkout serves the UAE and uses AED.
- Objects are designed in-house and made to order in the UAE. Do not narrow the confirmed production commitment to a particular emirate.
- The existing catalog, product pages, cart, checkout and contact flow provide the current implementation context; this record does not certify live payment or order fulfilment.
- Use current product records and policy content for specifications, prices, availability, lead times, returns and warranty terms. Do not introduce new promises from design examples.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the name Fasthaus and the existing approved brand assets.
- Preserve the connection between sculptural form, everyday function and warm light.
- Keep in-house design and UAE made-to-order production central to the product story.

## Product Principles

1. Prioritise the home shopper's path to purchase.
2. Help shoppers understand both the object's sculptural character and its everyday function.
3. Represent in-house design and UAE made-to-order production accurately.
4. Keep custom and trade enquiries accessible while maintaining the primary shopping journey.
